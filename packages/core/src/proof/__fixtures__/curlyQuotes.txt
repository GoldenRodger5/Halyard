A real App Store review, as the platform actually renders it.

The quotation marks below are genuine curly ones (U+201C, U+201D), not the
straight ASCII ones an editor substitutes. That distinction is the entire
reason this fixture exists: extractQuotes once had a character class written
with literal curly quotes that had been normalised to straight ones, so it
matched nothing and every quoted testimonial passed the gate unexamined.
