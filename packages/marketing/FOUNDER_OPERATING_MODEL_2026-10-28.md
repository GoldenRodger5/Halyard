# RecipeFix marketing operating model — founder decision, October 28, 2026

**Status: authoritative owner direction for planning; NOT an implementation or publication approval.** This supersedes earlier requirements that every creative be generated autonomously, that we use one narrator/stock-list template, or that posts auto-approve after a month of data. It does not supersede verified platform policy, source/rights constraints, account-ownership checks, or the existing rejection/hold on October 28 creative exports.

## What the founder actually wants

Build a **low-cost, varied, credible RecipeFix social presence** that gains an audience, community and ultimately active users and real subscribers. RecipeFix is the flagship product. The founder is willing to spend **one or two hands-on days approximately every two weeks** developing and approving **the next 14 days** of content. **Autonomous creative production is NOT the requirement.**

After batch approval, automate the routine operations wherever provider/platform capabilities allow:
- publishing/scheduling the exact approved files and copy at the approved times;
- recording post status, IDs, URLs, retries/duplicate protection, and notification-only steps;
- collecting available analytics, preparing useful reports, and bringing findings to the next planning session;
- recurring **market research**: discover relevant food/cooking conversations, trends, creators, channels, content styles, competitor posts, popular questions and comment themes, and worthwhile accounts/communities to follow or engage with.

The **research agent recommends actions**: what to discuss, where a real contribution would be helpful, which questions deserve an answer, and candidate comments/replies/DMs for founder review. It may organize, rank, draft, and report. **No automatic unsolicited DMs, mass commenting, follow/unfollow, disguised engagement, or spam.** Only conduct interactions permitted by each platform and authorized by the founder, with truthful identity and relevant value. Treat the founder's own inbound comments and mentions as higher-priority engagement opportunities.

## Channels and varied content

**Required publishing targets:** TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and X. Threads/Pinterest may be added after the four core channels work. One idea can have multiple platform-specific executions; do not equate a format variant with a new creative concept.

A two-week founder-selected mix should rotate naturally among:
- **What RecipeFix does:** tasteful intros, features, onboarding, differences from simple substitutions, and real product benefits;
- **Actual product walkthroughs:** real browser/iOS-app screen recordings (using safe test accounts/recipes), Adapt, serving changes, nutrition, Cookbook/versions, Cook, Plan, Grocery/Kitchen;
- **Recipe transformations:** show the original and the actual changes RecipeFix makes to ingredients AND method, with accurate estimates, honest tradeoffs and meaningful recipe visuals; never claim physical cooking footage or results that were not captured;
- **Food content beyond the app:** recipes, interesting ingredients, useful kitchen tips, historical stories, comparisons, seasonal topics and food discoveries;
- **Fun/community:** trivia, quizzes, food games, polls, this-or-that questions, humorous cooking situations, follow-up to real audience questions and comments.

Some posts explicitly introduce or promote RecipeFix; most earn attention or provide value without behaving like ads. We want shareable, enjoyable food content **and** a credible route to an app action. Do not overfocus on hidden-ingredient warnings, five-item lists, static captions, one visual motif, or mandatory narration. Rotate text posts/threads, image posts, carousels, polls, videos and genuine app-screen demos according to each platform's capabilities. No founder filming, personal narration, or hiring creators is required.

The October 28 owner rejection still applies to the current robotic Roger voice and generic darkened-footage/white-caption treatment. The seven held cuts are source material, not approved inventory. We may reuse facts, clips and components, not rebrand a rejected export as approved.

## The two-week collaboration rhythm

**Day 1: Research and select.** The research agent delivers a concise, source-linked brief with emerging conversations, high-signal content patterns, niche channels/communities, comments worth answering, candidate engagement actions, seasonal opportunities and what succeeded/failed in the last cohort. The founder and Halyard choose the content mix, scripts/hooks and format directions together.

**Day 1–2: Produce and approve.** Use Halyard's existing research, claim checking, asset/rights tracking, rendering, app recordings, preview, caption/hashtag and QA capabilities. Iterate on actual media, with varied creative styles and no obligation to automate every editing decision. Present one calendar with final media, exact platform variants, caption, tags, links, account destination, local time, rights/AI-label disclosure and cost; founder may approve, reject or revise each exact post. Approval is bound to its immutable content/metadata hash.

**After approval: unattended distribution where supported.** Schedule the approved 14-day slate in the selected provider and monitor results. Public publishing may run without being manually triggered for each post **only after that exact batch/account/time approval**. Notification-only formats must be explicitly shown as pending human completion, never reported as automatically posted. Observe platform API/rate and disclosure rules, retry safely, avoid duplicates, and record public URLs/status.

**During the fortnight: monitor, learn and recommend.** Collect available post metrics and growth outcomes at consistent windows (e.g. 48h, 7d); summarize meaningful hooks, audience questions, shares/saves, watch/retention, profile visits, referral clicks, actual RecipeFix adaptations, signups and verified paid conversions where they can be linked. Do not invent conversions by merging different populations. Research opportunities to follow, comment or reply helpfully; the founder decides whether to engage. Return an actionable brief for the next batch rather than autonomously rewriting and publishing a new one.

**At the next 14-day session:** use results plus new research to choose a fresh, *different* set of content. Do not optimize solely for views if it does not translate into relevant audience/app actions.

## Tools, distribution and budget

**Halyard is the existing creation, research, evidence, QA, calendar/review and campaign-tracking home.** Keep its current code, media, runs and open PR/worktree. Do not build another creator, scheduler, marketing stack or generic agent framework.

**Blotato remains a priority option**, because its existing integration offers publishing/connected-account and social information/analytics capabilities relevant to this operating model. Investigate the exact presently permitted account inventory, subscription status, scheduled posting, engagement discovery, analytics and supported interactions. Do **not** replace it automatically with Buffer based on a price claim; compare alternatives only if necessary after real capability/pricing/account checks. Existing Blotato account defaults are not RecipeFix-verified (previously MomentCircuit), and the last provider access returned a subscription-expired response. Never publish through the wrong brand.

**Budget preference:** as close to $0 as practical, ideally at or below **approximately $20/month total recurring new subscriptions**. The founder is open to a useful ~$20/month provider if it materially reduces the manual burden and supports the four channels; no purchase is preapproved. Variable AI/media spend must be approved and capped separately. Reuse existing infrastructure and legal/owned/stock assets; no required on-camera shoot, creator hire, expensive video subscriptions or extra AI services.

If no single affordable provider can truly auto-publish all four channels, transparently propose the lowest-friction compliant hybrid rather than asserting support. Identify platform-specific automation and manual gaps with current official/provider evidence. Timezones and daylight-saving changes must be handled correctly.

## Measurement and safety

Treat content as a **means to community and users**, not merely a social-post quota. Existing analytics, UTMs, PostHog, first-party events and billing authority remain sources. Distinguish website visitors, anonymous/social attention, legitimate first recipe value, saved/returned cooks, subscriptions and real revenue; exclude QA and obvious automation where supportable. Reports should call out missing data and propose practical experiments instead of fake certainty.

Every published asset and engagement action must respect copyright/licensing, truthful cooking/product claims, privacy/consent, platform rules and AI-media labels. The founder owns creative approval and relationship-building choices. No scheduled batch, new account connection, subscription, posting, commenting or outbound DM is authorized by this decision document alone.
