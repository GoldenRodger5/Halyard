# RecipeFix Social Studio — Cinematic Visual Quality Reset

**Founder decision:** October 29, 2026. **Status:** research-backed creative requirement, not a new subscription, AI spend, account connection, public posting or approval.

## The failure we are correcting

PR #115's 11.5-second pineapple/gelatin film is accurate and technically reproducible, but the exported frames rely on a flat fuchsia background, illustrated scissors cutting a generic white protein grid, small clip-art pineapple/can and a largely unchanged ending. It looks behind current generative video quality, and the founder rejects this exact film as flagship marketing content. Simply varying type, palette, transitions or increasing Blender primitive geometry won't solve the visual asset problem.

The founder explicitly names **NotebookLM Cinematic Video Overviews** as a premium visual-art-direction reference. In March 2026 Google confirmed NotebookLM Cinematic uses Gemini 3 + Nano Banana Pro + Veo 3 to create richer, immersive scenes and animation with a model acting as creative director and selecting story/style/motion. This is a **benchmark**, not proof that one generated video goes viral on TikTok, nor a reason to subscribe to Google AI Ultra at $249.99/month.

References: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/notebooklm/generate-your-own-cinematic-video-overviews-in-notebooklm/ ; https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/co-create-slides-videos/

## New default production architecture

Use Halyard for source truth, editorial planning, asset tracking, storyboarding, actual RecipeFix app proof, review, native formats, exports and later scheduling. **Replace generic algorithmic asset illustration for hero shots with deliberately art-directed assets** using current image-generation and image-to-video tools when they materially improve the film. Remotion/FFmpeg remain valuable compositors and editors, not mandatory artists of every visual object.

Pipeline: sourced recipe/problem and audience → director defines narrative with actual moving events and payoff → coherent visual style frames / references and camera treatment → generate or license high-quality *hero* images → animate only necessary shots through modern image-to-video or realistic footage → composite real RecipeFix proof, exact text and branding in Remotion → narration/sound where valuable → inspect complete export visually at 390px, audibly when perception is available → founder reject/revise/approve → only then platform variant and eventual approved publishing.

Prohibit fake cooked or taste-test results, simulated customer quotes, unsupported measurements, fake ingredient labels, inaccurate allergy/nutrition claims, unsupported food chemistry, and hiding AI/illustrative provenance. AI scenes illustrating pineapple/gelatin should not be described as observed experiments. Audio waveform/transport checks are not actual listening approval.

## A finite watched proof-of-quality gate

**Before further bulk video creation**, produce one premium 10–20s food-science or product-related story from existing approved sources, using visibly different visual materials. PR #115 is the baseline for visual comparison, not the style template.

1. Select a strong concept with a relatable hook and genuine action; define 3–5 cinematic beats and practical payoff. For pineapple, a possible scene could visually illustrate the difference between fresh and canned pineapple interfering with gelatin *setting*, not fake already-set dessert liquefaction.
2. Make **2–3 coherent, detailed style frames** (realistic food macro photography or intentionally designed high-end illustrative world), with matching subject/lighting/color, not flat SVG scissors/fruit icons.
3. Animate **one 3–5s hero shot** with actual subject/texture/camera action using Flow free or an authorized low-cost I2V model. Compare with a good rights-safe real-footage treatment or code-animated alternative; never claim CGI proves a physical experiment.
4. Edit a full version with actual lively pacing, minimal readable overlays, evidence and a visually satisfying payoff; narration/sound should be real or independently reviewed for naturalness.
5. Compare **actual rendered video** at TikTok/Reels/Shorts 390px feed size muted AND sound-on. Judge visual richness, object continuity, physical motion, lighting/texture, typography, no uncanny morphs, correct science and audience attention. Reject if it still looks like an animated PowerPoint.
6. Only if the visual quality is a **large obvious improvement**, introduce those art-to-motion components into repeatable Halyard workflows. Do not commit to another subscription or code integration solely on promising sample images.

**Business check:** A premium video is necessary but not sufficient for organic growth. Every filmed concept must relate to cooks, demonstrate a useful point and fit the existing 50/30/20 social portfolio; measure social qualified visits → first successful RecipeFix adaptations → repeat use/verified subscriptions rather than call pretty content “viral.”

Do not let this video R&D block the already stronger salt/seasonal Instagram/X content from progressing to explicit founder review. Production and account verification can remain separate tracks.
