# RecipeFix Social Studio — Cohesive Film Art Direction
**Founder directive:** October 29, 2026. **Status:** mandatory creative correction for current Phase 2B/PR118 successors; not authorization for paid media or publication.

## Rejected construction pattern
**AI-generated food background → abrupt website screenshot or mobile UI inset → giant type card / small unreadable proof → generic brand outro.** This looks like unrelated components stitched into a deck, despite technically appealing individual shots. The original RecipeFix UI wasn't designed to be a 9:16 commercial film. Cropping, zooming or adding a glow to those screenshots is not the fix.

The founder explicitly wants **one coherent, unified, art-directed moving film**, rather than separate food footage, app components and text overlays. This direction supersedes earlier mandates that authentic product screen captures must appear *visibly* in the public video. Source-true behavior remains mandatory, but screen pixels may be used only as private evidence for the script/story and replaced with faithful, purpose-built representations for video.

## New visual and story language

**Use one cinematic world:** a consistent setting, food subject, camera and lighting treatment that changes through *motivated actions*; camera moves, wipes, focus pulls and transitions flow naturally from the cooking problem and its consequence. Generated footage can represent a cooking scenario, but must be clearly understood as an illustrative scene, not cooked/tested output.

**App's intelligence as the physical event:** instead of showing a little dialog with 4 → 6 selected, make the viewer VISUALLY experience the need for more portions, ingredient scaling, physical broiler limit and modified cooking sequence. Use the genuine October 21 RecipeFix 4→6 Dairy-Free record as the factual basis; render accurate quantity, method and result through specially art-directed text/graphics *within* that same scene. Never fabricate actual UI, cooking results, nutrition, allergy certifications or the claim of appliance vision. A stylized visualization of an actual result is permitted if it is not presented as literal app footage.

**Text is direction, not decoration:** reduce on-screen copy to a few **intentional short beats**, inside shot-specific negative space, choreographed with what is happening. Do not place large arbitrary caption blocks over the food, leave tiny persistent sourcing notes, or cut the film to full-screen explanation cards. If narration is present, concise synchronous subtitles should be 2–5 words at a time, positioned after checking Instagram/TikTok/Shorts safe zones, not across the visual subject. Avoid universal fixed-placement captions; adapt to each shot and keep them clear of platform UI. The video should still make sense muted. Detailed citations, source scope, generated-media descriptions and adapted-result verification belong in the social caption/alt text or private evidence packet where platform disclosure rules permit, not tiny always-on text across footage. Required AI labels still apply.

**Narrative spine, not a forced shot list:** one hook → visible cooking constraint → clear RecipeFix-informed transformation → payoff → short natural CTA. Example for the dinner story: guests increase, one broiler has finite capacity, food and cooking sequence adjust into two batches; “RecipeFix adapted the plan” emerges from the resolved physical story, not from a separate embedded site tutorial. Use source-accurate numbers only where essential; avoid explaining every setting.

**Motion and sound as one composition:** material detail, meaningful actions and natural pacing; voice-over or original/licensed sound should be conceived with the edit, not tacked on after the picture is locked. Previously rejected Roger voice stays rejected. Music and effects need actual audible review by a capable listener; audio meters alone don't certify taste.

## Concrete proposed finishing test

Create one short *preproduction animatic* or rough sequence sketch with all beats flowing inside one world BEFORE allocating further paid video calls. Compare the entire storyboard against the old cut and ask if a stranger would experience a story, not separate media formats. Then make one bounded final re-edit with existing approved generated/recorded assets. If more realistic action is essential, present an exact source-reference/motion generation plan and cost cap for founder approval; don't automatically spend.

**Review the actual continuous film:** mobile-sized full playback in context, first 2 seconds, transitions between scene and product information, no UI-card effect, food/action still focal, natural readability, no text cutoff or clutter, cohesive camera/palette, motivated final branding, accurate RecipeFix benefit. Critic should assess the whole film, not individual assets. If the result still looks like a slide deck, reject/pivot rather than adjust fonts again.

## Scope and operational guard

This is a **creative direction correction** to current PR118/Phase2B specialists, not another platform redesign. Keep Halyard, Remotion/FFmpeg, source receipts, real RecipeFix captured output, reviewed food scene, existing publisher/analytics safety gates and other team branches intact. Product screenshot footage may remain in the internal proof gallery and provenance, but is not mandatory in the final public edit. No live posts, account connections, new AI spending or launch approvals granted here.

The point is to make an **actual compelling commercial film** that uses the product's intelligence without revealing the stitching between ingredients, claims, caption design and the creative world.
