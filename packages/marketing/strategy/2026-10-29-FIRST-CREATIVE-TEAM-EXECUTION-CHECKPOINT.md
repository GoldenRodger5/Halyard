## Content, platform and quality contract

**Content type is not distribution format.** The 15-category registry differentiates product introductions/actual adaptations/feature magic/nutrition/weekly cooking/ingredient swaps, teaching/science/history/kitchen math/recipes, and games/trivia/humor/community.

**Four platforms:**
- TikTok: 9:16 short videos and photographic/photo carousels when actual provider supports.
- Instagram: Reels, original 4:5 feed carousels/photos, Stories when capability verified.
- YouTube Shorts: self-contained vertical videos with meaningful title/context; no pretend Shorts carousel.
- X: strong native text, image, thread, video or poll when supported and worth doing.

One idea may generate independent variants for some platforms, none for others. Variant assets/copy/alt text/sources/rights/destination must be recorded. Use the implemented socialStudioContract.ts structures where appropriate; its offline exact-approval hash is NOT authenticated founder signoff and is NOT currently wired to publishing.

**Premium acceptance:** Actual media must look, move and sound premium; first seconds are watchable, action genuinely progresses, real payoff; professionally directed food visuals, no generic AI-slop, no clipped type, cardboard animation or dreary deck scenes. When voice adds value, use natural expressive narration with sound-on quality judged by someone who can actually listen; waveform meters alone aren't subjective listening. Don't blindly reuse rejected Roger. Watch full export at 360/390px muted and unmuted, inspect dangerous transitions, captions/contrast/safe zones, test media integrity, legal rights and real cooking/product claims. If concept fails after a substantial revision, replace concept instead of doing six font tweaks.

**Commercial acceptance:** Product/recipe creative has truthful real RecipeFix input/output, relevant CTA and verified route to a useful first Adapt. Fun and teaching pieces earn relevant food audience and question/comment interest without forced “download now” slabs. Track actual social engagement -> tagged qualified visit -> real successful adaptation -> repeat use/save/Cook -> provider-backed Plus only where the data can be joined. Do not turn one known real payer into a claim that this campaign converted.
