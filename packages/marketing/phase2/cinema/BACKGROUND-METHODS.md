# Background video methods — current findings

The recommended candidate workflow is **a strong physical story → coherent high-quality style frames → short image-conditioned action → exact app proof and edit → independent whole-film critique**. A model, MCP or expensive subscription is not itself a creative solution. The licensed food proof improved materials but still failed the generic-fact-assembly test; its exact critique is preserved in `video/review/root-cinema-critique.json`.

## Google API changes that stale tutorials miss

Google's [current video guide](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/video) states the Veo preview endpoints were shut down on October 22, 2026 and directs Gemini API users to `gemini-omni-1.1-flash`; GA Veo remains a separate cloud platform route. The actual key's returned catalog agrees: Omni and image models are listed, not Veo. `api-readiness.json` records this read-only observation without keys or project identifiers.

The [current image guide](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation) distinguishes Nano Banana 2.1, 2 Lite, 2 and Pro. The existing Scrapbook implementation used Nano Banana 2; it is a working credential source, not proof that its old model choice is still best. [Official pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing) puts 2.1 image output around $0.0336/1K and Omni 720p video around $0.10/s, plus input/text. Those are usage rates, not a fixed monthly subscription or a promise of unlimited retries.

`google-experiment.py` is a small, default-nonbilling runner for the [exact $2 proposal](API_EXPERIMENT.md). It reads the authorized existing env file in memory, preserves per-request receipts, caps conservative reservations, uses one output per request, never retries a submission automatically and pauses after the two images for actual inspection before motion. It uses official REST, not scraped browser tokens, and adds no dependency, server, database or scheduler. The dry-run plan reserved $1.32 conservatively; no generation is triggered by preparing these files. Execution is complete after explicit capped approval and resume. No further generation is authorized; see usage-estimate.json and receipts.

## Selection criterion

[Artificial Analysis's I2V evaluation](https://artificialanalysis.ai/video/leaderboard/image-to-video) compares models using blind same-input preferences. It helps shortlist motion models, but its general ranking cannot establish food-specific continuity, audience retention or RecipeFix conversion. The actual creative test must reject morphing food, sliding/intersecting pans, unexplained action and a late/weak payoff even if the vendor or benchmark is impressive.

TikTok's [food/drink creative research](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/pl-PL/blog/elevate-your-food-and-drink-ads) and [creative guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/creative-codes) emphasize sensory food, an early hook and stimulation grounded in a coherent story. Their [Top Ads dashboard](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-use-the-top-ads-dashboard) offers actual high-performing ads and second-by-second relative metrics. These support testing visible action and a useful payoff; they do not validate a specific RecipeFix video or guarantee virality. Specific inspected creator examples are recorded separately by the editorial team, with evidence and current/evergreen limits.
