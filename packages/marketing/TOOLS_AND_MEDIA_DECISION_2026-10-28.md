## What every video review must cover

Watch the exported file through the opening, every cut and the ending, with audio and at phone size. Inspect the raw ingredient action: hands remain consistent, liquid flows continuously, utensils contact food, ingredients do not morph, no invented labels, quantities or cooking results. Check that the words match the visible action, captions do not cover the action, and the voice has pauses and emphasis that fit the idea. Check audio entrances/exits, dead air, abrupt changes, music rights and voice intelligibility. Keep generated-media disclosures where required.

Automatic dimensions, transcript, safe-zone and frozen-frame checks are supporting checks. They cannot establish taste, natural speech or real audience retention.

## Fortnightly collaboration

1. Review actual results and a few current comparable posts together. Choose a small mix of useful food action, one honest recipe/product demonstration and native text/feed posts.
2. Pick the concept and evidence before selecting footage or generating anything. Mark what is documented versus illustrative. Audition voice on a short script before narrating the batch.
3. Make a small edit, watch it, improve the weakest meaningful beat, then create platform variants. Keep a finite model/provider budget.
4. Owner reviews video, audio, captions, rights, links and exact RecipeFix accounts. Record approval against exact files; prepare the next14days together.
5. Publish/schedule only the approved set. Use existing UTMs and actual visits/adaptations/registrations/provider conversions where identity linkage supports them. Bring measured results to the next review.

No unattended creator/publisher or new marketing platform is needed to run this workflow.
