## Actual creative tools to evaluate

- Remotion (already installed in Halyard): flexible React video editing, kinetics, effects, app UI, brand motion and export. Its current license is free for individuals/small teams up to 3; verify later business eligibility. https://www.remotion.pro/license
- Motion Canvas (MIT): TypeScript-based animated comparisons, game reveals, food-history timelines, vectors, numeric experiments. Evaluate incremental quality against existing Remotion before importing. https://motioncanvas.io/docs/
- Satori (MPL-2.0) + existing Sharp: sharp social graphics, X screenshots/infographics, swipable quiz cards; requires deliberate visual art direction. https://github.com/vercel/satori
- Playwright video recording: truthful, real RecipeFix UI navigation/Adapt/Cook/Plan/Grocery demonstrations with safe non-customer fixtures. https://playwright.dev/docs/videos
- Tscaps caption engine (MIT; separate editor AGPL), Capite (MIT) and FFmpeg: expressive word/phrase emphasis ONLY when voice helps; no mandatory voice. https://github.com/francozanardi/tscaps and https://github.com/muneebkhan08/Capite
- Pexels licensed footage for general food support, never evidence of an actual product label, measured scale, personal testimonial, or physically cooked RecipeFix result. https://www.pexels.com/license/
- ComfyUI + Apache-2.0 Wan 2.2, plus hosted AI-video options for a bounded 2–5-second motion/scene experiment. Open weights still require GPU/compute and license review. Do not infer good food physics from vendor demos. https://comfy.org/workflows/model/wan/
- Postiz AGPL self-hosted as one alternative scheduler, NOT presumed cheaper overall; hosting and platform API credentials/approvals are still needed. https://github.com/gitroomhq/postiz-app

Existing Halyard already includes Remotion + Sharp, and app, recipe, list, quiz, history, versus, transformation, slides/carousel modules. Keep them available but do not treat them as mandatory templates.
