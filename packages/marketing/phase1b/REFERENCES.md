## Tool decision

Remotion provides the required frame-driven SVG paths, clipping, gradients, finite particles and camera choreography. GSAP/HyperFrames remains an optional authoring route already proved in PR104. Motion Canvas could simplify long interdependent teaching timelines, but this pair does not justify another renderer. Satori is suited to a later static carousel; it does not supply physical animation. No new package, browser, local model or GPU was installed. No paid AI scene was necessary for these original illustrated concepts.
