# The little explosion

Experimental original illustrated food story. `PopcornStory` is1080×1920,30fps,405frames/13.5seconds. The shared entry supplies local Inter fonts and root-authored sound. No narration, stock or generated media, UI mockup, product capability claim or paid call.

The persistent Illustrated label is essential. Water/vapor, starch distribution, hull strain and timing are explanatory simplifications. It is not footage or a physical simulation. Factual basis and reference-inspection limits are in [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md).

## Story and sound contract

The opening seed already vibrates. Camera and water movement open the same kernel into a cutaway. Water contracts as vapor movement accelerates; finite pressure fronts reach a swelling hull. A crack precedes one decisive release atframe250/8.333seconds. The interior starch perimeter expands continuously into one asymmetric butterfly contour; two hull rims separate and fall away. Folded recesses and a small retained hull resolve into the food payoff. The ending explains what became fluffy.

Exact sound intentions are in [audio-cues.json](audio-cues.json). The root producer owns sound generation, synchronization and final mix; this component neither fetches nor plays independent audio.

## Substantial revision after actual v1 export

V1 was17seconds. Root and independent visual review identified a long, near-static cutaway; too many changing caption blocks; a popcorn silhouette that resembled cauliflower; and a scale/fade replacement instead of visible material continuity.

V2 removes3.5seconds, reduces the explanatory caption phases, advances water/camera entry, visibly links vapor motion to hull strain, and changes the rupture to an aligned72-point interpolation of the original authored kernel/starch contour into the original authored popcorn contour. This is a deterministic geometric explanation, not a scientific model. The new hull separation and folded payoff address the mechanism rather than changing fonts. V1 exports remain preserved in the external build directory.

V2 actual playback still exposed two eye-like gold fragments inside the pale expanding form, and a uniform radial crease pattern that suggested a flower. The bounded V3 source revision removes all four small central fragments and the surrounding rim arcs. Two curved hull pieces now originate at the outside edges and travel farther outward than the expanding starch. The food surface uses three large, separately shaded asymmetric folded wings over a deep warm cavity, plus a forward lower fold and short edge highlights. The timing stays405frames with the pop at250. V3 requires an actual rendered review; source/type checks cannot establish improved food recognition.

## Engineering and known creative limits

All positions, paths, opacity, pressure waves and finite fragments derive from `useCurrentFrame()`. No timers, external requests, random state, animation loops, new runtime dependency or GPU model. Path perimeter samples were generated once from our own original SVG geometry using installed browser SVG APIs, then checked into the component as constants.

Standalone TypeScript validation and Git whitespace checks passed. That is not creative approval. Remaining risks include the deliberately stylized food rendering, explanatory labels and whether the physical event is engaging enough at phone size. No founder/publication approval is implied. Actual final export review belongs in the shared review board.
