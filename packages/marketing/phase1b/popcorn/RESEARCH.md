## Popcorn factual contract

Verified [Popcorn Board explanation](https://www.popcorn.org/All-About-Popcorn/What-Makes-Popcorn-Pop) and [American Chemical Society educational PDF](https://www.acs.org/content/dam/acsorg/education/whatischemistry/adventuresinchemistry/secretscience/popcorn/secretscience-popcorn.pdf). Both describe moisture/starch inside a strong hull, heat turning internal water into vapor, pressure breaking the hull and starch expanding into the fluffy part. ACS distinguishes irregular butterfly popcorn from rounder mushroom popcorn. Our payoff uses an asymmetric butterfly form.

The animation is labeled Illustrated. It simplifies spatial arrangement, shape changes and time; not microscopy, an exact simulation, a literal free water droplet claim or a measured temperature/pressure demonstration.
