Why these two

One asks you to track and anticipate a comic reveal; the other follows a physical cause toward an explosion. We rejected a third concept about a growing dinner party because its chairs, ingredient carts and method-proof metaphor became cluttered and returned to the old product story. These are different experiences, not renderer comparisons.

Facts, illustration and exact media provenance

The game is original fiction: it teaches no food-safety, dietary or cooking-outcome claim. The yolk stays owned by the same shell throughout. The popcorn story is an explanatory illustration, not real cooking footage, a measured simulation or RecipeFix output. It simplifies microscopic structure and timing.

Popcorn facts are checked against the American Chemical Society's Secret Science of Popcorn and Popcorn Board explanation: kernels contain water and starch; heat and pressure lead to hull rupture; expanding starch forms the fluffy part. No temperature, nutrition, exact expansion ratio or universal cooking instruction is claimed.

All food shapes, surfaces, characters, motion and sound in the films are original procedural assets authored locally through AI-assisted code for this experiment. No stock, competitor footage, image/video-generation service output, synthetic voice, publisher recipe photo, or newly fabricated app result is used. Local Inter fonts carry their bundled SIL Open Font License. Reference films were watched privately for craft and are excluded from these outputs.

Sound and review limitations

Sound has a narrative role: plucked rhythm follows the game, stops before the reveal and resolves; the story uses rising pressure texture, anticipation, one synchronized rupture and a decay. These are original synthesized effects, not recordings of food.

The review tools can inspect playback, waveform, loudness, peaks, timing and stereo structure. They cannot directly hear musical taste or timbre. No claim that an agent listened or that the soundtrack is appealing is made. Please use the sound-on buttons for that decision.

Production tools, cost and decision

Existing Remotion, React, original SVG geometry, frame-driven paths, gradients, masks, deterministic particles and FFmpeg. No new renderer or dependency was needed; HyperFrames, Motion Canvas and Satori were considered for the scene rather than installed by default.

$0 additional provider/media spend; $0 new subscriptions. Local compute and editing time are not priced as free labor. No paid model calls, GPU models, account changes, RecipeFix production changes, posts or scheduling occurred.
