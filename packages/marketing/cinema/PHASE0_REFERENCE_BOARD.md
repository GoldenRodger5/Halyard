## Baseline failure and new direction

The owner-rejected dairy export's actual1/7/15s frames and the seven-piece contact sheet were reviewed in the prior closeout and remain unchanged. Motion happened under text, while the viewer's understanding barely changed. Repeated dim overlays, identical centered layouts, warning lists and recap endings had little visual payoff. Robotic Roger narration is rejected. A technical pass or AI rating does not change that.

New23s story: show a vivid cream transformation immediately; enter the actual RecipeFix UI; reveal the actual heavy-cream→coconut-cream change; show the associated real instruction note to whisk separated coconut cream smooth and maintain a gentle simmer; finish with a clear RecipeFix invitation. No nutrition claim, no invented cooked result, no paid generation and no narration.

A will use a warm editorial stage, carefully lit product planes and controlled camera moves. B will use more graphic, spatial HTML/GSAP match cuts. Both use the same source assets and evidence, the same length and sound bed. Actual footage/capture provenance is a separate acceptance gate. Phase0 is sufficient to begin the bounded two-render experiment; further reference research stops here.
