## Three approaches to the selected problem

**A — Broiler Tetris: select.** Start with an already crowded illustrated tray in a single broiler. A4→6 change increases the ingredient quantity while the physical stage stays the same. The extra pieces refuse to fit; the constraint is visible before a feature explanation. Actual current RecipeFix clicks select dairy-free and six; its recorded method becomes the intervention. The same illustrated material redistributes across two sheets and takes turns through the same rack. Finish on the useful changed cooking plan, then a short invitation to the actual Original→Makeitfit flow. No pretend cooked/tasted result, measured appliance detection or promise of saved time.

**B — The invitation keeps growing: kill.** Additional place settings arrive around a food photo and the app becomes a kitchen dispatcher. This revives the earlier guest-list/metaphor clutter; it spends attention establishing dinner props rather than resolving the cooking constraint. Do not render a backup by default.

**C — One-thumb rescue: kill.** A full-screen parameter counter makes recipe rows spill into an oven, then a camera zoom finds the answer. Too dependent on UI reading and decorative spilling; risks another proof-card presentation. Actual interface is evidence inside A, not a substitute for a story.
