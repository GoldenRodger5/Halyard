## Honest critique of this batch

The earlier selected dairy/gluten/kitchen exports were accurate and readable, but visually repetitive: dim food, centered sentences and the same composition for every fact. Stock motion improved the opening, yet the rest still resembled a reading exercise. The close repeated information rather than revealing a new result. Generic comment questions could not supply the missing demonstration. All three fresh model reviews remained `revise`; they were not proof of audience appeal.

The other four candidates remain held for concrete reasons: obvious cheese weakens the vegan surprise and microbial rennet does not make dairy vegan; halal questions need method and religious context; swaps differ in function and are not universal ratios; label claims need jurisdiction and allergen context. These are visible in each candidate's editorial notes.

## Changes applied to the existing pipeline

- Native list videos preserve the food action above a lower caption area. The ingredient name, important word and explanation now have visibly different roles. The key word is no longer buried inside a sentence. No boxes, paper cards, outlined novelty type or sound effects were added.
- X receives a separately composed 1200×675 image, useful text and sourced replies. Instagram gains 1080×1350 feed slides. These are compositions from the same verified facts, not cropped vertical videos or a new publishing system.
- The existing stored-result transform becomes a proof-led native video: the broiler-space change is stated immediately, the verbatim method comparison follows before the dairy swaps, and a useful batch instruction closes it. The original food photo is licensed and labelled; no image is represented as the adapted dish or a tested result.
- Its X companion compares the exact original and adapted excerpts at feed size. Synthetic-photo/voice disclosures and exact-creative approval remain enforced by the existing publisher.
- Preview videos are cached by the exported video's SHA-256, preventing a repaired export from retaining an older preview. Owner choices bind to the exact creative digest. The review script is emitted once, after all candidates exist.

## Editorial decisions before the next render

Use the existing brief and review record, not a new agent or scoring service. State: the viewer's specific problem; what is visibly demonstrated; what the source actually proves; the first payoff; one reason to save/share; the native platform treatment; and whether the food is illustrative or documented. If the visual cannot demonstrate the claim, change the concept or label it accurately rather than adding animation to imply proof.

Our next qualified proof concept is the stored tikka method change. Our list videos remain a useful educational hypothesis, with generated stills openly disclosed. A creator cooking the original and adapted versions would supply stronger physical proof, but that footage does not yet exist and cannot be fabricated.

After exact account/batch approval, use real retention and completion for videos, saves/shares/replies for feed posts, and existing UTM attribution through actual successful adaptations. Compare the three pieces before increasing volume. Public competitor counters and model scores do not predict RecipeFix conversions.
