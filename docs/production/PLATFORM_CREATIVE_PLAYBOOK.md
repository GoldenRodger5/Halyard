# Halyard Platform Creative & Social Playbook

This document defines how Halyard should reason about each destination. It complements `HALYARD_PRODUCTION_PROGRAM.md` and must remain current with provider/API reality.

Platform rules fall into three categories and must be labeled accordingly:

- **Platform fact:** verified capability/constraint from provider documentation or live capability check.
- **Heuristic:** editorial/distribution assumption used before account-specific evidence exists.
- **Halyard empirical:** account/product-specific finding supported by Halyard observations.

Never present a heuristic as measured fact. Never claim a trend is current without a current source.

## Shared rules

1. One idea does not imply one universal export.
2. Reuse source assets and strategic ideas; adapt the final execution when platform culture/surface/objective differs.
3. Diversity means different premise/story/proof/creative approach, not merely a font/color swap.
4. Preserve successful recognizable series when evidence supports them; avoid forced novelty.
5. Product evidence must be real. Stock/generated atmosphere cannot prove product behavior.
6. Keep clean media masters/stems for destination-specific audio/native finishing.
7. Each platform variant records why it exists, what differs, and whether reuse or a fresh edit was chosen.
8. Platform-native engagement is useful only when relevant and permitted; Halyard must be willing to recommend no action.

# TikTok

## Content families

- real-action product demonstration;
- transformation/before-change-after where evidence exists;
- search/question-led answer;
- creator/founder-style explanation;
- original adaptation of a relevant format/trend;
- comment/question response video where appropriate;
- recurring series;
- tips/process/how-to;
- eligible photo/carousel formats where supported.

## Creative direction

Do not hard-code "TikTok = frantic." Choose pace from the concept. Strong TikTok work often establishes the premise immediately and gives visible reasons to continue. For product content, the product action should appear early when it is the proof.

Use meaningful motion: hands/actions, app interaction, changing states, process footage, reframing, kinetic text, or spatial explanation. A moving background is not meaningful motion by itself.

Native/trending audio may be referenced only through rights-compliant workflows. Do not download another creator's sound into cross-platform exports.

## Discovery inputs

Where permitted/available:

- TikTok Creative Center trend/search data;
- Creator Search Insights/native search-gap observations;
- current search/web results;
- operator watch terms;
- creator/account observations;
- comments/questions on owned posts.

Store source, region/language, timestamp, observed metrics, and uncertainty.

## Acquisition

CTA/destination must match actual TikTok capability for the account and app. Do not invent clickable destinations. Demonstration-led content should show value before asking for action.

## Engagement

Owned-post replies/questions are distinct from inserting the product into unrelated conversations. Any automated reply path must be policy/API eligible and human-approved under Halyard's action policy.

# Instagram

## Content families

- Reels;
- product demonstrations;
- polished transformations/comparisons;
- educational carousels;
- single-image announcements/visual explainers;
- Stories using only supported real native interactions;
- behind-the-scenes/brand personality;
- FAQ/objection answers;
- comment response content.

## Reels

Optimize around the specific idea: save/share value, product clarity, entertainment, or education. Use strong cover/frame selection, readable captions, visual hierarchy, and coherent brand treatment.

Do not force TikTok's exact edit onto Reels. Reuse may be optimal sometimes; otherwise adjust opening, pacing, cover, text density, audio, caption, and CTA.

## Carousels

The cover earns the swipe. Each slide adds a distinct useful step/contrast/proof. Avoid empty slides, repeated title cards, tiny type, and decorative filler. Product carousels should show real screens/results where appropriate.

## Stories

Distinguish rendered story graphics from real native polls/questions/links. Never imply an interactive element exists if Halyard can only render a picture of one.

## Engagement

Prioritize owned-account comments/questions, customer objections, and relevant community conversations. Reply drafts must use the actual thread context and product truth.

# YouTube Shorts

## Content families

- concise answers to search/intention questions;
- product demonstration;
- mini tutorial;
- myth correction;
- comparison;
- transformation;
- recurring educational series;
- derivative Short from long-form when the excerpt stands on its own.

## Creative direction

Strong retention structure matters, but do not reduce every piece to the same pacing. The opening should make the premise clear and the payoff worth staying for. Search-oriented Shorts should answer the query honestly rather than stretching a simple answer.

Destination planning must respect YouTube's real link surfaces; do not write a CTA assuming links in Shorts descriptions/comments behave like a normal clickable funnel.

# YouTube long-form

## Content families

- tutorial/how-to;
- product walkthrough;
- case study with supported evidence;
- comparison;
- educational explainer;
- experiment/demonstration;
- deep dive;
- recurring series.

## Production requirements

- dedicated long-form concept and narrative architecture;
- authored chapter structure;
- multiple scenes/shots within sections;
- real product footage or legitimate supporting media;
- voice/music/SFX appropriate to long-form pacing;
- graphics/lower thirds/callouts where useful;
- thumbnail candidate set;
- title/description/chapter metadata;
- derivative Shorts opportunities.

Never stretch a short-form beat into a minute-long held scene. Every section should contain enough authored material and visual progression for its duration.

## Thumbnail system

Generate multiple materially different candidates; score small-size readability, focal clarity, curiosity, title complementarity, brand fit, and recent repetition. The selected thumbnail is a first-class asset with lineage.

# Pinterest

## Content families

- evergreen visual guides;
- step-by-step graphics;
- comparison cards;
- transformation/recipe/product result where rights permit;
- image/video Pins;
- search-led educational pins;
- destination-led feature examples.

## Creative direction

Attribution-required imagery may only be used when the source licence and destination format permit the exact usage and attribution/link-back is retained.

# X

## Content families

- concise insight/observation;
- supported product announcement;
- thread/explanation;
- annotated image/demo clip;
- relevant question/poll where native capability exists;
- founder perspective;
- eligible contextual reply.

## Creative direction

Text should carry the thought; media supports it. Do not force every post into a marketing CTA. Links/cost/rate-limit behavior is part of the delivery plan.

## Replies

Eligibility and automation policy must be checked at send time. A relevant post is not automatically permission for an automated reply. Prepare useful drafts and explicit manual/native fallback where required.

## Content families

Prioritize a natural conversational voice. Avoid overproduced launch-copy syntax and hashtags as a substitute for substance. Threads variants may reuse the underlying idea while rewriting for conversation rather than captioning a video.

# Reddit / community surfaces

Reddit or other community discovery is conditional on authorized/provider-compliant access. The system may research public discussions where allowed, but must not assume it can post automated promotional replies. The primary value is discovering real language, recurring pain points, questions, and content gaps.

# Trend → content workflow

1. Observe a current signal.
2. Normalize/store source, platform, time, metrics, region/language, and rights state.
3. Determine whether it is a trend, question, format pattern, creator pattern, seasonal event, or isolated post.
4. Check relevance to the product's audience and goals.
5. Check whether Halyard has an original/useful contribution and the evidence/assets to make it.
6. Check recent account history for repetition/fatigue.
7. Generate materially different concepts.
8. Produce platform-native variants only where worthwhile.
9. Review/repair actual media.
10. Human approval.
11. Schedule/deliver.
12. Measure outcome and feed learning.

Halyard can output `monitor`, `ignore`, `reply`, `create evergreen follow-up`, or `create trend-timed content`. `Make a post` is not the mandatory answer.

---

# Conversation → reply workflow

1. Capture the exact conversation and target identity.
2. Determine whether the product/account has a legitimate reason to respond.
3. Fetch enough surrounding context to avoid answering the wrong thing.
4. Draft the useful answer first; product mention/link is optional.
5. Show exact target, account, reply text, evidence, and platform eligibility.
6. Human approves.
7. Re-fetch/recheck target state and eligibility.
8. Send once through the permitted route.
9. Reconcile remote response/uncertain outcome before any retry.
10. Record relationship/opportunity outcome and useful content ideas that emerge.

# Creative diversity model

Track recent history at product/account/platform scope across:

- audience problem;
- premise;
- editorial format;
- concept family;
- proof/evidence type;
- hook family;
- narrative structure;
- media type;
- visual language;
- typography;
- opening;
- motion grammar;
- product-demo timing;
- voice;
- music attributes;
- CTA/destination;
- duration;
- series identity.

Reject superficial diversity when the underlying information/story is the same. Permit recognizable series and repeated successful treatments when evidence supports continued use. Use exploration deliberately rather than randomizing every design element.

---

# Platform acceptance requirement

A platform is not considered production-ready because an adapter exists. For every declared surface Halyard must have:

- capability definition;
- authenticated account state where applicable;
- real destination constraints;
- creative brief rules;
- render/copy path;
- review path;
- approval path;
- delivery/manual path;
- remote status reconciliation;
- metric collection or explicit unsupported state;
- representative finished output reviewed at mobile/feed size;
- evidence that shared changes also work for RecipeFix, Kinolog, and the third real validation app.
