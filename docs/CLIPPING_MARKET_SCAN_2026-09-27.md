# Clipping Market Scan & Operating Economics — 2026-09-27

## Purpose

This is a fresh market/economics register for the clipping operation. It is not an income forecast.

Campaign inventory changes quickly. Recheck live status immediately before production.

## Platform mechanics: Content Rewards

Current provider facts:
- CPM, per-post and retainer campaign models exist.
- CPM creator fee: 10%.
- Per-post / retainer fee: 10% below $5,000 campaign budget; 0% at $5,000+.
- CPM clips earn verified views for 7 days after approval, followed by a 3-day validation hold.
- Per-post payouts settle shortly after approval; V2 documentation states about 15 minutes.
- Approved clips only are paid.
- CPM campaigns can impose minimum and maximum payout per clip.
- Post link must be submitted shortly after publishing; current creator page states within 30 minutes.
- Fraud/bot scoring and human brand moderation occur before settlement.
- Withdrawal from Content Rewards to Whop has no Content Rewards withdrawal fee/minimum; downstream Whop settlement timing can differ.

Do not count:
- posted but unapproved clips;
- unvalidated views;
- reserved budget;
- pending payout;
- gross campaign CPM before the 10% fee;
as received cash.

## Current campaign-ranking model

Rank active campaigns on:

1. **Startability**
   - no established-follower requirement;
   - no niche/audience-history requirement a new account cannot satisfy;
   - assets reachable;
   - no prior portfolio gate.

2. **Expected economic ceiling**
   - CPM;
   - max payout per clip;
   - remaining budget;
   - fee;
   - minimum payout threshold.

3. **Competition**
   - listed clippers;
   - amount already spent;
   - recency;
   - saturation.

4. **Creative fit**
   - obvious visual hook;
   - multiple editable source moments;
   - ability to create distinct edits;
   - English/Tier-1 audience compatibility.

5. **Platform breadth**
   - TikTok;
   - Instagram;
   - YouTube Shorts.

6. **Operational friction**
   - pre-approval required;
   - dedicated-page requirement;
   - minimum account age;
   - audience demographics;
   - link-in-bio requirement;
   - broken asset host.

## Creative best practices grounded in current platform/provider guidance

### Hook
TikTok and YouTube both emphasize the opening seconds.

Operational rule:
- first visual should already contain motion/payoff/conflict;
- no logo sting;
- no "hey guys";
- no slow context setup;
- text hook appears on frame 1 where the brief allows it.

### Payoff first
For a source moment with a clear reaction/payoff:
- tease/show the payoff immediately;
- then give enough context to understand it;
- land on the reaction/result.

### One clip, one idea
Do not cram four unrelated moments into a 25-second cut unless the campaign specifically wants a montage.

### Cut dead air hard
Remove:
- greetings;
- repeated setup;
- filler words when they do not add personality;
- silence;
- irrelevant transitions.

Do not remove pauses that create comedy/tension.

### Captions
- high contrast;
- readable mobile size;
- synchronized;
- do not cover key UI/faces;
- correct names/brand terms;
- campaign-approved wording overrides our default style.

### Retention learning
YouTube's own retention tooling recommends moving later "top moments" earlier and inspecting spikes/replays.

Our equivalent:
- log first-second hook;
- log 3s hold when available;
- average watch;
- completion;
- shares;
- follows;
- views by 1h/6h/24h/7d;
- use winning moment families earlier in future edits.

### Authentic variation
YouTube's reused/inauthentic policies and brand briefs both punish low-value duplication.

Therefore:
- distinct source moment or distinct storyline;
- meaningful edit changes;
- do not clone one clip across multiple accounts;
- do not industrially generate near-identical versions.

## Earnings math

### CPM clip
At $2 CPM:
- 1,000 approved views = $2 gross;
- 10,000 = $20 gross;
- 100,000 = $200 gross;
- 150,000 = $300 gross if clip cap is $300.

With Content Rewards' 10% CPM creator fee:
- $200 gross campaign earnings corresponds to $180 after the CR fee, before personal taxes and downstream payout fees.

This makes **view quality and volume** the real lever, not editing hourly rate.

### Portfolio economics

One clip is lottery-like.
A repeatable portfolio is the business.

The operating goal is:
- reduce production cost per high-quality attempt;
- retain enough creative variance to discover winners;
- increase the percentage of clips that cross campaign minimum payout;
- identify sources/hooks with repeatable reach.

Never model earnings as CPM × hypothetical views without labeling the result as a scenario.

## First experiment

After owner setup:
- join one clean launch campaign;
- publish 6–9 genuinely distinct clips over roughly 72 hours, subject to campaign cadence/rules;
- preserve every zero-view/failed result;
- compare hooks/source moments;
- pivot before increasing volume.

First real milestone:
**first approved clip with validated payout.**
