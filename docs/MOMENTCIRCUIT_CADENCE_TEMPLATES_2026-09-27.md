# MomentCircuit Cadence & Video Template Policy — 2026-09-27

## Launch cadence

Start with **2 new master videos per day** for the first 10–14 days.

Cross-post each eligible master to:
- TikTok;
- Instagram Reels;
- YouTube Shorts.

That means roughly:
- 14 TikToks/week;
- 14 Reels/week;
- 14 Shorts/week;
- but only 14 unique master edits/week, not 42 separate productions.

This is deliberately aggressive but not spammy.

External benchmark:
- Buffer's analysis of 11.4M TikToks found higher posting frequency increased the odds of breakout performance; 11+ posts/week had the highest average lift they measured, while median views stayed relatively flat.
- Metricool's 2026 study found TikTok content volume rose sharply and per-post performance fell, reinforcing that quality/precision matter more than raw volume.

## Initial time slots

Use:
- **13:00 ET** — exploration slot;
- **20:00 ET** — primary slot.

8 PM is supported by Metricool's 2026 TikTok study as the strongest aggregate hour.
1 PM is deliberately an experiment bucket, not claimed to be universally optimal.

Do not post two variants of the same video back-to-back.

## Escalation to 3/day

Move to 3/day only after 10–14 days if:
- production quality remains unchanged;
- no evidence that the extra slot depresses average retention/follow conversion;
- backlog supports genuinely distinct posts;
- paid campaign inventory or organic winners justify the volume.

Third slot begins as **16:30 ET** and remains experimental.

If quality slips, return to 2/day.

## Template system

### 1. Growth Cut
- 15–35s typical;
- vertical 1080x1920, 30fps;
- immediate frame-one action;
- top safe-zone hook;
- large source-video region;
- lower-third captions;
- one payoff;
- no intro animation;
- campaign brief overrides every style choice.

Use for:
- campaign clips;
- streamer reactions;
- gaming moments;
- fast celebrity moments.

### 2. Story Cut
- 60–90s typical;
- vertical 1080x1920, 30fps;
- original narration/context;
- top safe-zone hook;
- main source/B-roll region;
- lower-third transcript captions;
- small context label;
- clear payoff/end beat.

Use for:
- "what happened here?";
- creator lore;
- context behind viral moments;
- native-originality monetization path.

### 3. Episode / multi-part
Use only when the story has real structure that cannot be told cleanly in one piece.

Rules:
- 2 parts preferred; 3 max by default;
- every part must have its own hook + mini-payoff;
- never cut mid-sentence purely to force Part 2;
- small unobtrusive "Part 1/2" marker;
- Part 2 must make sense if discovered first;
- both parts are produced before Part 1 publishes;
- publish Part 2 in the next appropriate slot, usually 6–24h later;
- if Part 1 dramatically outperforms baseline, prioritize Part 2 earlier;
- if Part 1 fails, Part 2 may be repackaged as a standalone video rather than blindly posted.

TikTok has explicitly expanded micro-series/episodic storytelling products in 2026, supporting the broader format, but MomentCircuit uses multipart only when the story earns it.

## Cross-platform packaging

Keep the master visual language consistent, but export/publish platform-specific metadata.

TikTok:
- prioritize immediate hook and native feel;
- avoid text under right-side controls/bottom caption UI;
- Personal account.

Instagram:
- 9:16;
- keep important text in safe zone;
- Creator account.

YouTube Shorts:
- same 9:16 master can work;
- title is packaging, not just caption;
- track Engaged Views and retention, not raw starts alone.

## Safe-zone / quality standard

TikTok official creative guidance:
- full-screen 9:16;
- high-resolution media;
- account for UI safe zones.

Meta's Reels guidance likewise recommends 9:16 video with key messages in safe zones.

Minimum MomentCircuit export:
- 1080x1920;
- 30fps unless source strongly benefits from 60fps;
- no blurry upscales;
- no watermarks;
- captions clear on phone;
- no key text behind UI;
- normalized dialogue audio;
- no dead air in opening.

## Learning loop

Track by post:
- slot;
- template;
- duration;
- hook family;
- niche;
- campaign/organic;
- 1h/6h/24h/7d views;
- retention/watch time;
- completion;
- shares;
- followers;
- payout;
- policy/copyright flags.

After enough data, replace the 1 PM exploratory slot and even the 8 PM anchor with account-specific best-performing windows.

The schedule is adaptive, not sacred.
