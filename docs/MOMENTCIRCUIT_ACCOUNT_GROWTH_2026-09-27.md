# MomentCircuit Account Architecture & Growth Playbook — 2026-09-27

## Decision

**Do not use one TikTok account for literally every campaign.**

Use one primary account for every campaign that fits one coherent audience promise.

Primary brand:
**MomentCircuit — high-retention internet, gaming, streamer, creator, and pop-culture moments.**

Campaigns that fit this audience can share the same TikTok / Instagram / YouTube identity.
Campaigns that require a different niche, audience, profile identity, or demographic history are rejected or moved to a separate account only after the primary account proves traction.

This is a business rule, not a claim that TikTok requires one niche per account.

## Why

TikTok says the For You feed is personalized from interests and engagement, including watching, liking, commenting on, and sharing similar posts. A coherent account gives viewers and the recommendation system repeated signals about who should enjoy the next post.

Content Rewards also requires creators to describe their content/audience truthfully, and says using an audience that clearly does not match campaign targeting breaches its Creator Terms.

Therefore campaign CPM is not enough. The campaign must fit the account.

## MomentCircuit audience promise

A viewer should know why to follow after seeing any three posts.

Allowed core topics:
- streamer moments;
- gaming moments;
- gaming launches / culture;
- creator/influencer moments;
- music/celebrity moments when the edit is about the moment/story rather than generic advertising;
- internet culture / viral creator stories;
- short "what happened here?" entertainment explainers.

Tone:
- entertaining;
- fast;
- curious;
- non-corporate;
- visually immediate;
- not mean-spirited/rage-bait by default.

Not MomentCircuit:
- toy unboxings / kids-product pages;
- beauty-only campaigns;
- crypto/finance;
- gambling;
- generic ecommerce products;
- bodycam/police footage;
- synthetic horror/found footage;
- unrelated local-business ads.

Those are different audiences.

## Campaign fit gate

A campaign may publish on MomentCircuit only if all are true:

1. **Brief fit**
   - campaign allows a general page or MomentCircuit satisfies its page requirement;
   - no dedicated-page naming/profile requirement that conflicts with MomentCircuit;
   - no follower-age/audience-demographic claim we cannot truthfully demonstrate.

2. **Audience fit**
   - reasonable overlap with gaming / creators / music / celebrity / internet culture;
   - a normal MomentCircuit follower could plausibly enjoy the post even if it were not paid.

3. **Creative fit**
   - source material has a real hook/moment/story;
   - campaign allows enough editing to make the piece feel native;
   - required wording does not turn the feed into obvious repetitive ads.

4. **Economics**
   - budget remains meaningful;
   - source assets are reachable;
   - minimum payout is realistic for the current account;
   - payout cap justifies the production effort.

5. **Rights/compliance**
   - supplied/authorized source;
   - all campaign disclosures and tags can be followed;
   - no prohibited engagement manipulation.

If any fails, reject the campaign.

## Examples

### Fits MomentCircuit
- CoCo Jones campaign: creator/celebrity moment storytelling, no dedicated-page requirement in the brief.
- Call of Duty campaigns: gaming culture and launch moments, assuming source assets work and page requirements permit.
- DiegoLoveless / Twitch creator campaigns: direct streamer fit.

### Does not fit MomentCircuit
- My Mini Mixed Capsule: brief requires toy/unboxing/miniature/kids-content theme pages or UGC creators plus audience requirements. Do not distort MomentCircuit to chase its $2 CPM.

## Account-growth system

The feed cannot be only paid campaign posts.

**Initial operating mix (hypothesis, to be measured):**
- 60% organic / unpaid but rights-safe MomentCircuit posts;
- 30% paid campaign posts;
- 10% experiments.

This is not a TikTok platform rule. It is our anti-spam/account-value policy.

### Organic formats

1. **Moment of the day**
   - one creator/gaming/pop-culture moment;
   - fast context;
   - payoff.

2. **What just happened?**
   - 45–90 sec original narration;
   - explain the context behind a viral moment.

3. **Chat lost it**
   - creator/streamer reaction;
   - frame the audience reaction as the hook;
   - use authorized/licensed source.

4. **You missed this**
   - overlooked detail/context;
   - commentary-led.

5. **Three moments**
   - tightly themed roundup, only when rights basis permits all excerpts.

6. **Original AI/graphic explainer**
   - our narration + original graphics/video;
   - useful when no third-party footage is needed.

Do not scrape and repost random social media clips merely to fill the 60%.

## Creative standards

TikTok's current business guidance repeatedly emphasizes:
- vertical 9:16;
- high-resolution footage (720p+);
- native/entertaining style;
- a strong hook in the opening seconds;
- hook → body → close structure;
- text/captions that aid viewing;
- continued testing and creative refresh.

Our stricter standard:
- motion or emotional information in frame 1;
- no logo intro;
- no dead air before the hook;
- viewer understands the premise quickly;
- readable captions;
- one primary payoff;
- campaign-required text always overrides our template;
- never add trending music when a campaign forbids it.

## First-account launch sequence

### Before first post
- anonymous-account OPSEC complete;
- profile image + bio look like a real media page;
- at least 6 posts are prepared so the page does not remain a one-post shell;
- at least 3 posts are non-campaign posts so the account is not born as an ad feed.

### First 7 days
Operating hypothesis:
- 2–3 strong posts/day if we can maintain quality;
- no mass burst of near-identical posts;
- test different hook families and source moments;
- campaign rules/cadence always win over this hypothesis.

A new account is not judged from one upload. Evaluate a body of distinct posts.

## Analytics loop

For every post store:
- topic cluster;
- creator/game;
- campaign vs organic;
- hook family;
- first line/on-screen hook;
- duration;
- posting time;
- views at 1h / 6h / 24h / 7d;
- likes;
- comments;
- shares;
- saves when available;
- followers gained where available;
- average watch time;
- completion/retention;
- campaign approval;
- qualified/approved campaign views;
- payout;
- policy/copyright issues.

We are trying to learn:
- which audience cluster follows;
- which hook earns the first seconds;
- which source type creates shares;
- which format converts views into followers;
- which campaigns earn without weakening organic account performance.

## Growth vs revenue conflict

A high-CPM campaign can still be bad for MomentCircuit if it attracts the wrong audience.

When revenue and audience quality conflict:
1. do not damage the main account for a one-off payout;
2. reject the campaign;
3. only create another niche account if the campaign class has enough repeated inventory to justify a durable page.

No disposable account farm.

## When to open account #2

Do not open another page just because one campaign does not fit.

Open another niche only when:
- at least 3 repeatable campaigns/source streams exist in that niche, or;
- an owned-content format shows strong repeatable organic traction, and;
- the additional account can be fed without reducing MomentCircuit quality.

Planned separate brand:
**Incident / bodycam stories** — only after rights sourcing is durable.

Optional later:
**fictional AI found-footage** — separate account because viewer expectation is fundamentally different.
