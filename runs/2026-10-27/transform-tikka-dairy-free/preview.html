Dairy-free tikka masala, scaled from 4 to 6 servings. Swapping the yogurt, butter and cream is the easy part. What changes in the broiler step? Guess before the answer.

Recipe Fixed #1: Real RecipeFix result, captured 2026-10-21. Recipe: a RecipeFix Original.

3/4 cup plain whole-milk yogurt → 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons plain unsweetened dairy-free coconut yogurt
Why: Coconut yogurt provides an acidic, coating marinade without dairy; choose an unsweetened, unflavored variety.

2 tablespoons butter → 3 tablespoons vegan butter
Why: Vegan butter supplies the fat needed to soften the onion and bloom the spices without dairy.

1/2 cup heavy cream → 3/4 cup full-fat coconut cream
Why: Coconut cream restores the sauce's richness without dairy; unsweetened soy cooking cream offers a less coconut-forward alternative.

The step: "Broil the Marinated Chicken". As written: "spread it on a foil-lined baking sheet … Broil for 8 to 10 minutes". Fixed: "spread in a single uncrowded layer on two foil-lined baking sheets … broil one sheet at a time for 8 to 10 minutes". Why: Allow two broiler batches at this size, keeping the cooking time per batch unchanged; let excess coconut yogurt drip off so the coating chars rather than pools.

• For 6: two sheets, broil one at a time, 8 to 10 minutes each.
