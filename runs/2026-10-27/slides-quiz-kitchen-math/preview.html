Could you scale a recipe without a calculator?

3 questions. Answers in the reply, no peeking.

#cookingtips #bakingtips #recipefix

thread:

1. Answer: B) 3

1 tablespoon is 3 teaspoons, or half a fluid ounce.

Source: USDA Food Buying Guide, Volume Equivalents
2. Answer: C) 4

A quarter cup is 4 tablespoons. A full cup is 16.

Source: USDA Food Buying Guide, Volume Equivalents
3. Answer: C) 5 and 1/3

One third of 16 tablespoons is 5 and 1/3: that's 5 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon.

Source: USDA Food Buying Guide, Volume Equivalents
