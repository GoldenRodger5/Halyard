The seven-post critique

PostWhy a viewer can leaveStronger concept and required evidence
Hidden dairyNo package or ingredient list is shown. Coffee to steak is a topic jump, not a developing story.Use one real creamer package: front claim → turn bottle → reveal milk protein in its actual ingredients. Do not imply the current stock glass contains that product.
Hidden glutenThe soy sauce pour never proves wheat content. Another warning arrives before the first has a visual payoff.One bottle and its actual wheat declaration beside a verified gluten-free alternative. Physical label close-up, not invented AI packaging.
Kitchen weightsThe images do not show either cup being weighed. Text supplies all the substance.One locked camera shot: tare the same cup, fill and weigh each ingredient, reveal the actual measured values. Use 120g/198g only as attributed chart values, not fabricated scale readings.
Not veganA broad five-item warning list, obvious dairy imagery and a long recap dilute the useful detail.One specific label investigation with a visible ingredient and an appropriate replacement. Drop the five-item list.
HalalA sweeping headline hides method, certification and religious context; stock footage cannot settle it.Hold. Only make a narrowly framed ingredient/certification explainer with qualified review and clear context.
SwapsThe promise is universal while the ingredients serve different functions. No texture or cooking result is shown.One recipe and one function: show the original and changed method, then document both real outcomes before making texture claims.
Label rulesUnrelated rules and jurisdictions turn the post into a reading task. No particular decision is resolved.One real package, one jurisdiction, one practical interpretation. Make native X text plus a cropped annotated evidence image.

Production recommendation

Make one proof-led piece before another batch. The most credible next shot is hands turning a real package to its ingredient label, or a filmed kitchen comparison. Own or commissioned footage provides the action, result and human detail our library lacks. Stock remains useful for supporting shots; it cannot supply the missing proof.

For voice, keep a continuous performance and edit the pictures to it. If synthetic speech keeps failing the bar, use a real human read. ElevenLabs Voice Changer can preserve an authorized human performance while changing its voice; that is a testable alternative, not a guaranteed fix. Do not clone another creator or use competitor footage.

Use Halyard for factual briefs, rights, exports and exact owner review. Use an ordinary timeline editor for the pilot. Descript's private project exists but AI editing is blocked by its plan credits; no upgrade was purchased. Higgsfield's model catalog is not proof that generated food or people look credible. No AI video was generated or claimed to pass review.

For a fortnightly workflow: choose concepts together, secure the actual demonstration media, approve one finished video and voice, then make native feed/X variants and a small batch. Do not convert every fact into a narrated list or schedule unapproved output.
