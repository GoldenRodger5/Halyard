One swap can move the whole method. In this dairy-free chicken tikka masala, scaled from 4 to 6 servings, the ingredient list is simple: coconut yogurt, vegan butter, coconut cream. The steps are where cooks get burned. More chicken needs more broiler space, not a longer blast. A wider pan means you start checking the sauce at 10 minutes and stop when a spoon dragged through leaves a clear trail. Coconut cream wants a gentle simmer. Chicken still needs 165°F in the thickest pieces. This is a real RecipeFix result, so the changed step comes with the reason.
