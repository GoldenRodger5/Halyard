# MomentCircuit Launch Pack — Brand Profile

## Brand
**MomentCircuit**

## Positioning
The moments everyone is talking about — gaming, streamers, creators, and internet culture, with the context that makes them hit.

## Handles
Use first available in this order:
1. @momentcircuit
2. @momentcircuit.media
3. @momentcircuitclips
4. @themomentcircuit

Try to keep TikTok / Instagram / YouTube identical.

## TikTok bio
**Gaming • streamers • creator culture**
**The moments everyone talks about.**

## Instagram bio
**Gaming • streamers • creator culture**
**Viral moments + the context behind them.**

## YouTube description
MomentCircuit covers the moments shaping gaming, streamers, creators, and internet culture — fast clips, context, reactions, and the stories behind what everyone is talking about.

## Public identity rules
- no founder name;
- no personal photo;
- no personal voice;
- no location;
- no employer/school;
- no LogicAndLayers link;
- no personal social links;
- no public login/recovery email.

## Avatar direction
Dark, simple, recognizable at 40px:
- circular circuit-ring mark;
- centered MC monogram;
- no face/mascot;
- no copyrighted character;
- works on black/white backgrounds.

## Feed promise
Every post should answer one of:
- What just happened?
- Why did everyone react like that?
- What did you miss?
- What happens next?
- Why does this moment matter?

If a post cannot satisfy the feed promise without its campaign payout, reject it.
