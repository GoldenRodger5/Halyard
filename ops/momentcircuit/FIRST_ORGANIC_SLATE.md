# MomentCircuit — First Organic Account-Building Slate

These are original commentary concepts. They do not rely on ripped third-party footage.

Purpose: give the account an identity beyond paid posts.

## Organic 01 — Why viral clips became a business

Length: 45–75s

Hook:
“Those random streamer clips all over your feed? There’s an actual industry paying people to make them.”

Beats:
1. Streamers/brands intentionally distribute moments through clipping.
2. Campaigns pay based on approved views or fixed posts.
3. Clippers are effectively performance distributors.
4. Audiences discover creators through moments, not full streams.
5. Close: “The clip is becoming the trailer for internet culture.”

Visual plan:
Original kinetic typography + original abstract livestream/UI visuals. No ripped creator footage required.

## Organic 02 — Why ‘the moment’ beats the full stream

Length: 35–60s

Hook:
“Nobody needs to watch a five-hour stream to know the one moment everyone will talk about tomorrow.”

Beats:
1. Live streams create huge amounts of raw material.
2. One reaction/conflict/reveal becomes the unit people share.
3. Context makes a clip understandable.
4. MomentCircuit finds the moment and explains it fast.

Visual plan:
Original motion graphics / waveform / chat-style UI.

## Organic 03 — Warzone’s new skin toggle, explained

Length: 45–75s

Hook:
“Warzone is adding a switch that changes which operator skins you see — but it doesn’t delete anyone’s skins.”

Use only Call of Duty’s 24 Sep 2026 official announcement:
- Season 1 of MW4;
- Warzone only;
- optional toggle;
- replaces non-MW4 Operators with MW4 Operators in the viewer’s game;
- your own equipped Operator is unchanged;
- toggle is off by default;
- Carry Forward remains.

Visual plan:
Original typography/diagram only until official campaign footage source is restored. Do not use unofficial gameplay or ripped trailer footage.

## Organic 04 — The two-video rule

Length: 45–60s

Hook:
“One viral moment can actually be two completely different videos.”

Beats:
1. 20–30s growth cut = instant payoff.
2. 60–90s story cut = context, narration, why it matters.
3. Short version earns discovery.
4. Story version builds the brand and supports original-content monetization.
5. Close: “Same moment. Different job.”

## Organic 05 — Why context is the new clip

Length: 45–75s

Hook:
“The clip gets the view. The context gets the follow.”

Beats:
- raw moment;
- what happened before;
- why viewers care;
- what happened after;
- why fandom/story explainers drive repeat viewing.

## Initial publishing sequence

Do not launch with six text-only explainers.

Once accounts are ready:
1. strongest CoCo campaign edit;
2. Organic 02;
3. second CoCo edit;
4. Organic 03 if COD is still fresh;
5. third CoCo edit;
6. Organic 01;
7. next approved gaming/streamer/creator campaign.

This gives the feed faces/moments plus an original editorial identity.
