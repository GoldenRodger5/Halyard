# CoCo Jones Campaign — First Production Slate

Source of truth: current ClipFarm campaign brief + supplied WeTransfer package.

## Hard rules
- supplied campaign footage only;
- no old footage;
- tell a story;
- remove dead space;
- distinct edits, not collab-spam;
- authentic/raw tone;
- no negative framing;
- no fake/botted engagement.

## Candidate 1 — How they met
**Source:** “How They Met”

Hook options:
- “They met at a party… but neither remembers the details 😂”
- “Their love story started with a very blurry memory 😂”
- “Coco & Donovan trying to remember how they met 😭”

Edit:
1. Start on the funniest/most uncertain line.
2. Cut setup before the actual story.
3. Preserve reaction/laughter.
4. End on the cleanest payoff.

Caption:
Coco and Donovan trying to piece together how they first met is too real 😂🥂
