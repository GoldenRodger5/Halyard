# MomentCircuit Pre-Publish Quality Gate

This is the release gate for every MomentCircuit video. A render can exist without passing this gate; a post cannot.

## 1. Rights and campaign truth

Must be true:
- the source has a documented rights basis;
- the campaign is still accepting clips at publish time;
- the source asset host is reachable;
- the exact campaign brief has been reviewed;
- required disclosure, tag, caption, platform, duration, and music rules are satisfied;
- no edit implies facts, chronology, or quotes unsupported by the source.

**Any unknown here blocks publication.**

## 2. Opening quality

The first frame must already contain information or motion.

Reject:
- logo stings;
- fades from black;
- “hey guys” setup;
- dead air;
- a hook that only becomes understandable several seconds later.

Target:
- premise understandable immediately;
- first meaningful beat within ~1 second;
- first 2–3 seconds create a reason to continue.

This is a quality target, not a fabricated platform threshold.

## 3. Edit quality

Must pass:
- 9:16 master;
- 1080×1920 export when source supports it;
- no visible watermarks from editing tools;
- no blurry crop or avoidable upscale;
- faces/action remain framed;
- no dead space that weakens pace;
- cuts do not distort what was said;
- one clear story/payoff per Growth Cut;
- Story Cuts have a beginning, escalation/context, and payoff.

## 4. Captions and text

Must pass:
- spelling and names checked;
- captions synchronized enough to read naturally;
- high contrast;
- mobile-readable size;
- no important text under right-side/bottom platform UI;
- hook text does not obscure faces/action;
- campaign-approved wording overrides house style.

Do not use giant captions merely because they are common on low-quality clip pages.

## 5. Audio

Must pass:
- dialogue is intelligible on phone speakers;
- no clipping/distortion;
- source audio remains natural unless the brief permits treatment;
- no music when a campaign forbids it;
- added music/SFX have a documented usage right;
- no unnecessary AI narration over a moment that works better in source audio.

## 6. Native feel

The video must look like a strong native short-form edit, not an exported ad template.

Reject:
- repetitive identical layout across every post;
- fake reaction stickers;
- random gameplay under unrelated dialogue;
- excessive emoji/SFX;
- unnecessary progress bars;
- obvious AI filler;
- forced “follow for part 2” bait.

## 7. Multi-part rule

Only use multiple parts when the story genuinely benefits.

Each part:
- has its own hook;
- contains a real mini-payoff;
- makes sense if discovered independently;
- is produced before Part 1 publishes.

Default:
- 2 parts preferred;
- 3 maximum unless evidence supports more.

## 8. Brand/account fit

Before accepting a paid campaign post, ask:

> If this campaign paid $0, could a normal MomentCircuit follower still enjoy this?

If no, it does not belong on the main account.

## 9. Anonymity

Check:
- no personal name;
- no personal email/notification/UI;
- no location or identifying screenshot;
- no personal voice unless intentionally anonymized;
- no cross-brand identifiers;
- metadata from personally captured media stripped when relevant.

## 10. Final phone check

Before posting the first example of any new template/source family:
- watch the entire export at phone size;
- listen once without headphones;
- verify hook text/captions are not covered by platform controls;
- verify no accidental freeze/black frame;
- verify final frame does not linger awkwardly.

## 11. Learning tags

Attach:
- niche;
- source/campaign;
- Growth / Story / Episode;
- hook family;
- duration;
- publish slot;
- campaign vs organic;
- experiment id.

Without those tags, the later analytics cannot teach us what actually worked.

## Release verdict

Only three states:
- **READY** — all blocking checks pass;
- **HOLD** — quality/brief issue can be fixed;
- **STOP** — rights, campaign, account, or factual uncertainty means do not publish.

There is no “good enough because we need volume.”
