Gummy middle? Wrong diagnosis.
A gluey gluten-free crumb usually gets blamed on the flour blend.
Sometimes it's just underbaked.
Gluten-free starches need enough time at the right temperature to finish setting, and five minutes early can be the difference between gummy and done.
So people start swapping ingredients when the oven timer was the real problem.
A thermometer settles it faster than another trip to the pantry.
