Four people asked me the same question in 30 days.

All four had the same problem: gluten-free bread that turns gummy in the middle. All four sent me their own recipe first.

I kept trying to fix the recipe. Swap this flour, add that binder. It didn't land, because that wasn't what they were asking.

By the fourth message I finally heard it. Nobody wanted a new recipe. They wanted someone to look at the one they already had and say what broke. That's a different question, and most recipe content doesn't answer it. It hands you steps, not a diagnosis.

That gap is why I started building this.
