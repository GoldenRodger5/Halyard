Your gluten-free bread isn't undercooked.

That gummy strip down the middle happens because the crumb keeps setting as the loaf cools, not just while it bakes. Starches need that resting time to finish absorbing moisture and firm up. Cut it warm and you're tasting an unfinished process, not a mistake.

Here's the part that throws people off: two slices from the exact same loaf, one cut ten minutes out of the oven and one cut after a full cool, look and feel like different bread. Same bake. Same recipe. Different slice.

If you've been adding flour or extra bake time to fix gumminess, the loaf might not be the problem. The clock is.
