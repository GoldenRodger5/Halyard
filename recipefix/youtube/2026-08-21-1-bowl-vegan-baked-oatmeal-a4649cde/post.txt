Your baked oatmeal middle is gummy

This vegan baked oatmeal bakes in an 8x8 pan sized for 4 servings. That depth gives the soft, creamy texture the recipe is built for. A bigger pan spreads the batter too thin and it overbakes before the center ever sets.

The flax egg needs its full 5 minute rest to gel. Skip that and you're left with a runny binder instead of something that holds the casserole together.

Vegan butter does the same job the dairy version did here, keeping the crumb from turning rubbery.

At about 44% of the original batch, this version bakes faster than you'd expect. Start checking at 22 minutes, not the original 35, and pull it while the center still looks barely set. It firms up as it cools.
