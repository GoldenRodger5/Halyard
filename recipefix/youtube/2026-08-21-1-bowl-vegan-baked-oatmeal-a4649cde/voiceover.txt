Gummy middle isn't the recipe. It's the timer.

This vegan baked oatmeal bakes in an eight by eight pan for four servings. That depth gives you soft, creamy texture. A bigger pan spreads it too thin, and it overbakes before the center sets.

The flax egg needs its full five minute rest to gel. Skip that, and the binder stays runny instead of holding together.

Vegan butter keeps the crumb from turning rubbery, close to what dairy butter does here.

This smaller batch bakes faster. Start checking at twenty two minutes. Pull it while the center still looks barely set. It firms up as it cools.
