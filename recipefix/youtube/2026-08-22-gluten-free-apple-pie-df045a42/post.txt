Gluten-free crust keeps tearing.

Gluten-free flour blends can swap in for wheat 1:1 by volume, but not by behavior. No gluten means no elastic web, so dough cracks when you lift it. Chilling it first helps it hold shape, and pressing the cracks back together works fine once it's in the dish.

The filling behaves oddly too. Cornstarch replaces the flour thickener at half its volume, since it's twice as effective, but it only activates once it hits oven heat. The filling will look loose in the bowl. Wait for visible bubbling before pulling the pie. That's the sign the starch has set.

Building these adaptations, the recipe was never the hard part. Reading someone's description of what went wrong, like 'it collapsed' or 'it was weird,' and mapping that to an actual cause took longer than fixing any single pie.
