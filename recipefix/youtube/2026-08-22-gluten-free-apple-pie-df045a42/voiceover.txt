Gluten-free crust keeps tearing on you.

A gluten-free blend swaps in by volume, but it behaves differently. No gluten means no stretch, so cracks happen when you lift the dough. Chill it first. Press cracks back together once it's in the dish.

Cornstarch replaces flour in the filling, at half the volume. It only activates in the oven, so the filling looks loose in the bowl. Wait for visible bubbling before pulling the pie.

Honestly, the recipe wasn't the hard part. Figuring out what "it was weird" actually meant took longer than fixing the pie.
