Your gluten-free loaf isn't undercooked.

Starches in gluten-free bread keep absorbing moisture and firming up as the loaf cools, not just while it bakes. Cut it warm and the crumb still looks wet and dense, even past the point where the center hit a safe internal temp. The structure hasn't caught up to the heat yet.

That's the part people miss: doneness and "set" are two different clocks. The oven finishes one. The counter finishes the other.

Let a loaf rest fully before you judge the crumb. The knife will tell you a different story than the one it told twenty minutes earlier.
