Gummy middle usually means it came out too soon.

Gluten-free crumb can look done on the outside before the starches inside finish setting, so a loaf that's actually underbaked reads like a bad recipe instead.

The fix most people reach for first is swapping flours. That's the twist: changing the blend doesn't touch the real variable, which is how long the center had to finish cooking. A few more minutes in the oven costs nothing. A new bag of flour blend costs money and doesn't solve the actual problem.

Next time the middle comes out dense, check doneness before you touch the recipe.
