A substitution I suggested made a loaf worse, not better.

I swapped bread flour for all-purpose and left everything else the same, figuring protein content was a minor detail. It was not. The crumb came out dense and gummy instead of open and springy. My guess is the tool treated flour type as one variable among many when it should have driven the whole recipe. I did not catch the miss until the second bake, when I finally cut the liquid instead of leaving the ratio untouched.

Publishing the failure feels worse than posting a win, but it is the only way to show the pattern before it shows up in your kitchen.
