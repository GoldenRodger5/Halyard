Gummy center, wrong pan.
We scaled this vegan baked oatmeal from 9 servings down to 4, and the batter volume dropped enough that an 8x8 pan replaces the original 9x9 or 11x7. Less pan means a shallower batter, and shallower batter sets faster. The written bake time is still 25 to 28 minutes, but the real cue is to start checking at 22. Pull it on the old schedule and the middle looks gummy, not because the oats or the flax egg failed, but because it never finished setting. The flax egg itself costs you something too: skip its 5 minute rest and the gel stays thin, so the crumb loosens before pan size ever enters the picture.
