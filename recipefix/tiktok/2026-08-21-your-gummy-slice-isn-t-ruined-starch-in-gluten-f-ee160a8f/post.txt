Your gummy slice isn't ruined.

Starch in gluten-free bread keeps setting as it cools, well after the loaf is fully baked. Cut it warm and the crumb still reads wet, even though it's done.

Wait it out and the same loaf turns tender instead of gummy. No extra step, no fix. Just time you didn't know you needed.

Cut too soon and you'll blame the recipe every time.
