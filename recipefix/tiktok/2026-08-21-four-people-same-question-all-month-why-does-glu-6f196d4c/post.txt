Four people. Same question.
All month: why does gluten free bread turn gummy in the middle. Four different people, same complaint, same recipe.
By the fourth message I stopped answering with tips. I asked what happened, step by step. That's when I saw the real gap. Nobody wanted a new recipe. They wanted to know what broke in the one they already had.
That gap is the whole reason this exists.
Next time your loaf sinks in the middle, don't hunt for a new recipe. Ask what broke first.
