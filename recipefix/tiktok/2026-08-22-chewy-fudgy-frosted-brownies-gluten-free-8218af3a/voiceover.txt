I stopped rewriting the whole recipe.

One swap: flour becomes a gluten-free blend, ingredient five. That part reads as a straight substitution.

The real changes show up lower down. At quarter scale, the pan drops from nine inches to a six-inch square or a loaf pan. A nine-inch pan spreads this batter too thin and bakes a wafer, not a brownie.

Watch it closely too. Gluten-free batter can brown faster, so check with a toothpick starting at eighteen minutes.

Small, marked changes. People trust the ones they can see.
