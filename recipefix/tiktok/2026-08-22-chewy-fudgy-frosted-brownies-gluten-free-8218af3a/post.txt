I stopped rewriting the whole recipe.

One swap: the all-purpose flour becomes a gluten-free blend, ingredient five. That part reads as a straight substitution, not a rewrite.

The real changes show up lower down. At quarter scale, three eggs becomes one egg, or the batter turns rubbery instead of fudgy. The pan drops from nine inches to a six-inch square or a loaf pan. A nine-inch pan spreads this batter too thin and bakes a wafer, not a brownie.

Small, marked changes. People trust the ones they can see.
