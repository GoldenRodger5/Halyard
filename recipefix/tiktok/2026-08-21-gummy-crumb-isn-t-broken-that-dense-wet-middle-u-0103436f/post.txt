Gummy crumb isn't broken.

That dense, wet middle usually isn't a bad flour blend. It's starches that never finished setting before the loaf came out.

Gluten-free crumb depends on starch gelatinization to hold structure, and that process keeps happening even after the crust looks golden. Pull it on visual cues alone and you catch it mid-transformation.

Check the center instead of the clock next time.
