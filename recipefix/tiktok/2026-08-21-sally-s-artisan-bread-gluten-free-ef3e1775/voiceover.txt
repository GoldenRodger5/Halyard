Your gummy bread isn't undercooked. Gluten-free dough looks like thick batter, not shaggy dough. Don't add flour to fix it. Keep the rise short, sixty to ninety minutes. There's no gluten to develop. More time just tires the yeast. Bake at four hundred fifty degrees. Give it thirty five to forty minutes. Gluten-free browns fast but sets slow. A hot oven risks a raw center. Let the loaf cool completely before slicing. Cutting early traps steam that looks like gumminess.
