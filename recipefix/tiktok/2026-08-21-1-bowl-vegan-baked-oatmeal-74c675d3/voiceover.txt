Your gummy loaf isn't broken. It's pulled too early. Mix one tablespoon ground flaxseed with three tablespoons water. Let it rest a full five minutes before it binds anything. This batch is scaled for four servings, baked in an eight by eight pan. It cooks faster too. Start checking at twenty two minutes, not thirty five. Pull it when the center looks barely set. It firms up as it cools. Skip that rest, and the crumb stays loose no matter how long you bake.
