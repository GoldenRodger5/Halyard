Gummy crumb isn't recipe failure.

In gluten-free loaves the starches need real time at full heat to finish setting after the crust already looks brown. Pull it when the outside looks done and the middle is still wet on a structural level, not an ingredient level.

Most people respond by swapping flours or adding more binder. The fix is usually just more time in the oven, or checking with a thermometer instead of a timer.

Still cutting into a wet middle. Back to the oven it goes.
