Watery filling isn't a mistake That's the cornstarch, not a mistake. It activates when it hits oven heat, not before, and it works at half the volume of the wheat flour it replaced because it thickens twice as hard. The tradeoff: gluten-free crusts brown fast, so start checking at thirty-five minutes instead of the full hour. Filling should be visibly bubbling before you pull it, that's how you know the starch fully activated.
